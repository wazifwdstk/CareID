      Find a family member

      For when someone is rushed to hospital with no ID and cannot speak (a Jane or John Doe), and their family does not know which hospital they are in.

      1. Hospital registers the unknown patient. Sex, estimated age, where they were found, clothing and features go into one registry shared by all hospitals.
      2. Hospital tries to identify them. Fingerprint checked against the MyKad record, or a CareID emergency card or phone lock-screen QR is scanned.
      3. Family search. Family describe the missing person here. CareID compares the description with unknown patients at every hospital.
      4. Confirm in person. The hospital calls the family to confirm identity. Then the normal family alerts start.
